## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an authoritative, tech-forward, and high-performance digital agency persona. Drawing inspiration from modern high-contrast digital craftsmanship and minimalist architectural UI, it blends deep ink-black environments with high-voltage neon coral accents. 

The aesthetic is precision-driven, sleek, and conversion-oriented. It conveys technical supremacy, engineering rigor, and refined aesthetic mastery tailored for global enterprise leaders and high-growth disruptors across North America, the Gulf, and South Asia. The experience avoids chaotic decoration in favor of razor-sharp hairline borders, luminous focal points, confident micro-interactions, and atmospheric dark-mode tonal layering.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an immersive deep obsidian background (`#0C0D12`) paired with CodeFier’s high-energy vivid coral red (`#FF4D4D`). 

- **Primary (`#FF4D4D`)**: Serves as the primary catalyst for conversion, interactive feedback, brand callouts, and key highlight states.
- **Secondary (`#94A3B8`)**: A neutral slate gray providing balanced hierarchy for secondary labels, metadata, and structural micro-copy.
- **Tertiary (`#1E2230`)**: Dark steel-indigo utilized for elevated interactive surfaces, chip fills, and contextual overlays.
- **Neutral (`#0C0D12`)**: The foundational deep obsidian space, creating infinite depth when contrasted against stark white headers (`#FFFFFF`) and warm soft white body copy (`#E2E8F0`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements an editorial yet technical interplay across three deliberate typeface assignments:

- **Display & Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers punchy, authoritative, geometric geometry with tight tracking for impactful agency messaging.
- **Body & Editorial (Inter)**: Provides maximum clarity and sustained reading comfort across case studies, proposals, and dynamic service specifications.
- **Technical Badges & Metrics (JetBrains Mono)**: Infuses raw engineering credibility, used exclusively for uppercase section indices, technical stack tags, status indicators, and client proof numbers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a standard 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by expansive, breathing section dividers. 

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12 columns with a 24px (`1.5rem`) gutter and generous 64px (`4rem`) outer margins. Content maximum width is capped at 1440px for cinematic readability.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8 columns with 24px gutters and 32px (`2rem`) canvas margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) edge safe padding.

Vertical rhythm relies on generous section pacing (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) to let large display headlines and rich project showcase cards maintain distinct agency prestige.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages crisp hairline outlines, subtle ambient luminescences, and deep surface layer stacking rather than muddy traditional dropshadows:

- **Base Canvas**: Solid `#0C0D12` background.
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards, Modules)**: `#12141C` paired with a delicate translucent outline (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Surface Level 2 (Flyouts, Hover States, Floating Nav)**: `#181B26` with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)` and a subtle ambient dark drop shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Coral Glow Accent (Focus & Interactive)**: Accent elements cast a diffused, neon coral aura (`0 0 24px rgba(255, 77, 77, 0.25)` and border transition to `rgba(255, 77, 77, 0.5)`), creating an illuminated, tactile response.

## Shapes

The design system employs a structured, contemporary rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`). Standard interface elements—such as agency project cards, text areas, and content wrappers—use 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radii to maintain discipline. 

Pill geometries (`9999px`) are intentionally reserved for primary CTA buttons, status indicator tags, and navigation anchors to draw the eye cleanly against rectangular content blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Conversion CTA)**: Fully rounded pill shape with solid `#FF4D4D` background and `#FFFFFF` bold typography. Hover triggers a subtle scale lift (`scale(1.02)`) and an ambient coral halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(255, 77, 77, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Ghost Outline)**: Dark translucent fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` and stark white text. Hover transitions border color to `#FF4D4D` with soft white text brighten.

### Cards & Case Study Tiles
- Built with `#12141C` background and razor-thin borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- Interactive cards feature an internal subtle radial gradient on hover tracking the cursor, bringing border lightness to `rgba(255, 77, 77, 0.35)` and lifting the card 4px vertically.

### Chips & Badges
- Styled with `JetBrains Mono` label text in all-caps.
- Background uses `#1E2230` with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Active/live badges include a pulsing emerald or neon coral dot (`6px × 6px`) to indicate real-time status.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- Dark structural wells (`#0F1117`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Text in `#FFFFFF` with muted slate placeholder (`#94A3B8`). 
- Focus state reveals an unambiguous `#FF4D4D` hairline perimeter border and faint atmospheric inner glow.

### Radio & Checkbox Selectors
- Squared with 4px border-radius for checkboxes, circular for radios. Base border is slate (`#94A3B8`), transitioning to vibrant `#FF4D4D` fill with clean white icons upon selection.

### Navigation Header
- Floating glassmorphic dock with backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), `#0C0D12/80` fill, hairline perimeter border, housing minimal navigation items and a high-contrast coral inquiry button.
